## About

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the University of Quebec
in Montreal, School of Management (ESG UQÀM). With a background in
quantitative methods for finance as well as actuarial sciences, I have
a keen interest in AI, machine learning, market modelization, volatility
dynamics, and state-space estimation methods for asset allocation,
risk management, pricing, and economic forecasting.

My current work focuses on:
- factor models
- neural networks for derivatives
- volatility term structures
- option surface modeling
